01 — chapters

Start a chapter.

Any student at any university can start a CompNeuroSociety chapter, and you do not need any connection to FSU or FIU to do it, so just email us and we will help you set it up.

02 — what we help with

Structure

We will show you how we organize board roles, membership, and meetings, and help with the paperwork side of registering a student org.

Curriculum

We share our workshop series and journal club formats, including what has worked for members with no coding experience.

Mentors

We can introduce you to faculty and graduate students in our network who advise project teams.

Project teams

We will explain how we pick papers to replicate and how we run a team over a semester, which you can see in our past projects.
