// fsu's computational neuroscience society

Hands-on computational neuroscience.

We are a student-run club at FSU, and we host workshops, journal clubs, and research projects so that students from any major can learn computational neuroscience by actually doing it. The brain on the right is an interactive model that shows EEG (a recording of the brain's electrical activity from the scalp), so feel free to drag it around.
